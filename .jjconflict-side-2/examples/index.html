x-ocaml examples

Each page shows one side of x-ocaml. Build with dune build at the root of the repository, then serve the repository's root directory, for example with python3 -m http.server, and open /examples/.

  • A short tutorial: prose, a hidden helper, exercises the reader writes, tests that check them, and the reader's work kept across reloads.
  • Running and stopping: long computations, one that never ends, and the Stop button.
  • Nothing runs until asked: run-on="click", and a different element name.
  • Theming: a dark theme through the --xo-* variables.
  • Text of all sorts: accents, emoji and other scripts, in code and in output.
  • Displays: elements a cell puts on the page that change as it computes and call it back, with X_ocaml_lib alone: a live estimate, controls with no JavaScript, a canvas, KaTeX, Mermaid and sound.
  • Drawing from the worker: a canvas the cell's code draws on directly, at the worker's animation frames.
  • Widgets: cells that draw controls and pictures and hear the reader's clicks and typing, on the worker with widgets built in widgets/.
  • A widget toolkit: Ui, the library in widgets/ui/: sliders, pickers, tabs, tables, plots, timers and interact, in the manner of ipywidgets.
  • Things made with widgets: the Game of Life, the Mandelbrot set, a curve fitter, Snake, a kaleidoscope and a harmonograph.

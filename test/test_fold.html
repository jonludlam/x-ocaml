A cell's fold attribute folds ranges of its lines behind their first line; its highlight attribute marks lines. Both count lines from 1 within the cell. A page that shows a long module and wants the reader to look at a few lines of it uses them together.

Folded ranges show their first line; marked lines are marked

module M = struct type t = | A | B | C (* the part to read *) let name = function | A -> "a" | B -> "b" | C -> "c" let one = 1 let two = 2 let three = 3 let four = 4 let five = 5 end let f1 = M.name M.C

Ranges that don't fit the source are left out

let f2 = 1 let g2 = 2

A fold opens when clicked

let f3 = 1 + 2
